01 / Currencies

Read the quotation first

A currency pair expresses one currency in units of another. In USD/INR, the quote describes rupees per US dollar. If the quote rises, a dollar buys more rupees: the rupee has weakened against the dollar in that quotation. Inverting the pair reverses the numerical direction.

  • The first currency is the base and the second is the quote.
  • Pip and tick conventions depend on the instrument and venue.
  • A cash conversion, an exchange-traded derivative and an offshore leveraged CFD are different products.
02 / Currencies

Currencies reflect relative conditions

Interest-rate expectations, inflation, trade flows, capital flows and risk sentiment can all influence exchange rates. These factors interact and can already be reflected in price. A higher local interest rate does not mechanically guarantee a stronger currency.

  • Expected policy changes can matter more than the announcement itself.
  • Commodity prices can affect importing and exporting economies differently.
  • Central-bank communication and intervention are sources of uncertainty, not predictable trading rules.
03 / Currencies

Indian residents must use permitted routes

RBI guidance states that residents may undertake forex transactions only with authorized persons for permitted purposes. Electronic forex transactions must follow applicable rules for authorized electronic trading platforms or recognized exchanges. The existence of a foreign website is not authorization to use it.

  • Check RBI authorization, the permitted purpose and your eligibility—not merely an advertisement or app-store listing.
  • Do not assume offshore forex or CFD funding is allowed.
  • Recognized-exchange products also have eligibility, documentation and contract requirements.
  • Consult current RBI and exchange guidance before any real transaction.
04 / Currencies

Study contracts without importing the wrong assumptions

Currency futures and options have their own quotation, quantity, expiry and settlement rules. A chart labeled EUR/USD elsewhere is not automatically the same product available through an Indian broker. Holidays and international sessions can alter liquidity.

  • Verify exact contract specifications and broker support.
  • Include spread, costs and rollover or funding where applicable.
  • This chapter explains concepts and does not recommend a pair or venue.
