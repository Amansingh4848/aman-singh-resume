Android

Chrome → Install app

  1. Open in Chrome. Use your Android phone’s Chrome browser, not the browser inside Instagram or WhatsApp.
  2. Install Trade Zuko. Tap “Install on Android” above. If Chrome offers an install prompt, confirm it. Otherwise, open Chrome’s ⋮ menu → Install app or Add to Home screen, then follow the prompt.
  3. Open your new app icon. Launch Trade Zuko from your phone’s Home Screen or app list and enter the password supplied by Aman.

Menu labels can vary by browser and system version. About browser installation ↗

iPhone / iPad

Safari → Share → Add

  1. Open in Safari. Visit this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Open Share. Tap the Share icon (square with an up arrow). In some Safari layouts, open the page menu first, then tap Share.
  3. Add to Home Screen. Scroll the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if it is shown, then tap Add.
  4. Open Trade Zuko. Tap the new Home Screen icon and log in with your app password.

iPhone and iPad do not offer a one-tap automatic install prompt. Menu labels can vary by browser and system version. Apple’s instructions ↗

GOOD TO KNOW

Same learning. Your screen.

  • Free to install. Installation does not enroll you in a paid class.
  • Password protected. Use the learner password supplied by Aman. Owner controls stay separate.
  • Connect to learn. The app’s opening screen can be saved offline; login, lessons and videos still need the internet.
  • Keep your notes. Your journal and progress are saved in the browser or app where you use them. They do not automatically sync between devices. Export important notes before removing the app.
