Android

Chrome → Install app

  1. Open in Chrome. Use your Android phone’s Chrome browser, not the browser inside Instagram or WhatsApp.
  2. Install Trade Zuko. Tap “Install Trade Zuko”. If Chrome offers an install prompt, confirm it. Otherwise, open Chrome’s ⋮ menu → Install app or Add to Home screen, then follow the prompt.
  3. Open your new app icon. Launch Trade Zuko from your phone’s Home Screen or app list and sign in with Google when available or your learner password.

Menu labels can vary by browser and system version. About browser installation ↗

iPhone / iPad

Safari → Share → Add

  1. Open in Safari. Visit this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Open Share. Tap the Share icon (square with an up arrow). In some Safari layouts, open the page menu first, then tap Share.
  3. Add to Home Screen. Scroll the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if it is shown, then tap Add.
  4. Open Trade Zuko. Tap the new Home Screen icon and sign in with Google when available or your app password.

iPhone and iPad do not offer a one-tap automatic install prompt. Menu labels can vary by browser and system version. Apple’s instructions ↗

TWO STEPS / ONE POCKET LIBRARY

Install the app. Download the knowledge.

  1. Add Trade Zuko to your Home Screen using the phone buttons above. On computers, Chrome/Edge may offer an install button in the address bar.
  2. Open the installed app while online and sign in with Google when available or your learner password.
  3. Choose “Download all lessons for offline use”. Choose a separate device password after Google sign-in, or confirm your current app password. Approve saving on your device, and wait for “Download complete”.
  4. No internet? Open Trade Zuko and choose “Open downloaded lessons offline”. Enter the password used for that download.

Includes 16 lessons, 32 patterns, 11 payoff structures, the candle/option-chain lab, course outlines and local study tools. YouTube/Instagram videos, new posts, WhatsApp and owner controls still need internet. Downloads are device-specific and may be cleared by your browser. Password changes cannot instantly revoke a device that stays offline.

GOOD TO KNOW

Same learning. Your screen.

  • Free to install. Installation does not enroll you in a paid class.
  • Separate access. Google sign-in is for learners only. A learner password is also supported. Owner controls stay private.
  • Learn offline after downloading. Saved lessons, charts and calculators work without internet. First login, fresh downloads, videos and password management need a connection.
  • Keep your notes. Your journal and progress are saved in the browser or app where you use them. They do not automatically sync between devices. Export important notes before removing the app.
